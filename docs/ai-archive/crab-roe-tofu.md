# 蟹粉豆腐 - Tofu with Crab Roe

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮经典菜式，选用鲜活大闸蟹现拆蟹粉，与嫩滑豆腐同烹，蟹香浓郁，入口即化。

A classic Suzhou dish made with freshly picked crab roe and meat from live 
hairy crabs, braised with silky tofu. Rich crab flavor, melts in your mouth.
