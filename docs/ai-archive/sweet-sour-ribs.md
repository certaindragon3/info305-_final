# 糖醋排骨 - Sweet and Sour Pork Ribs

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮经典菜式，精选肋排切段，油炸后裹上酸甜酱汁，色泽红亮，酸甜适口，肉质酥嫩。

A classic Suzhou dish with pork ribs cut into sections, deep-fried and 
coated in sweet and sour sauce. Glossy red color, balanced sweet-sour 
flavor, tender meat.
