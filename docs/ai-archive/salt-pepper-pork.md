# 椒盐排条 - Salt and Pepper Pork Strips

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮经典小食，精选猪里脊切条腌制，裹粉酥炸后撒上椒盐，外酥里嫩，香气扑鼻。

A classic Suzhou snack made with pork tenderloin strips, marinated, battered, 
and deep-fried until crispy, then seasoned with salt and pepper. Crispy outside, 
tender inside.
