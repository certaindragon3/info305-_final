# 银鱼炒蛋 - Scrambled Eggs with Whitebait

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮家常菜，选用太湖银鱼与土鸡蛋同炒，银鱼细嫩鲜甜，蛋香浓郁，简单却美味。

A home-style Suzhou dish with Taihu whitebait and farm eggs stir-fried 
together. Delicate sweet fish, rich egg flavor, simple yet delicious.
