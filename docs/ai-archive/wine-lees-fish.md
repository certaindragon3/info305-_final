# 糟溜黑鱼片 - Wine Lees Sliced Snakehead Fish

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮传统名菜，选用鲜活黑鱼片，以香糟调味，滑嫩鲜美，糟香浓郁，回味无穷。

A traditional Suzhou dish featuring fresh snakehead fish slices seasoned 
with wine lees. Silky texture, rich fermented aroma, unforgettable flavor.
