# 白什锦 - Assorted Delicacies in Clear Broth

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮传统冷盘，精选猪肚、蹄筋、鸡片等多种食材，以清汤慢炖，口感丰富，清爽不腻。

A traditional Suzhou cold platter with pork tripe, tendons, chicken slices 
and more, slowly simmered in clear broth. Rich textures, refreshing taste.
