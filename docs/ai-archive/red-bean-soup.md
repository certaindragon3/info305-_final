# 赤豆圆子 - Sweet Red Bean Soup with Rice Balls

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮传统甜品，以赤豆熬制浓稠汤底，配以糯米小圆子，甜而不腻，软糯可口。

A traditional Suzhou dessert with thick red bean soup and chewy glutinous 
rice balls. Sweet but not cloying, soft and satisfying.
