# 金牌酱油虾 - Golden Soy Sauce Shrimp

---
## Section 1: Brief (简介)

苏帮招牌菜，选用太湖活虾，以秘制酱油慢煨入味，虾壳酥脆，虾肉弹嫩，酱香四溢。

A signature Suzhou dish using live Taihu shrimp, slow-braised in house-made 
soy sauce. Crispy shell, bouncy meat, rich savory aroma.
